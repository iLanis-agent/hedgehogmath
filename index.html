Hedgehog math

A pet shop cage that looks generous can be half the floor a hedgehog actually uses, and the cute wheel next to it can be two sizes too small. Hedgehog math turns cage dimensions into a floor-area verdict, wheel diameter into a back-friendly check with a nightly lap count, and body weight into a food and bag plan. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Habitat check - cage length and width become a floor-area verdict.
  • Wheel check - wheel diameter becomes a size verdict plus laps per 5 km night.
  • Food plan - body weight and days become kibble grams, bags and leftovers.

Labeled norms

Habitat: 4 sq ft (0.372 m2) is the commonly labeled floor minimum; 8 sq ft (0.743 m2) is roomy. Wheels: the commonly labeled band is 28-32 cm so the back stays flat; solid running surface only - wire and bar wheels injure feet. Food: roughly 4% of body weight per day, about 1-2 tablespoons. Night runs: a 5 km night is the labeled activity estimate. Labeled community care norms, not veterinary advice - an exotic vet knows your hedgehog, these calculators do not.
